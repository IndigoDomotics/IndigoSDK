# Backlog

Future / not-yet-designed ideas for this repo — the "someday / maybe" parking lot. Not small
changes you're doing now (those go in `TODO.md`) and not designed work (that's a `planning/`
plan). See `dev-standards/backlog-format.md` for the format and the graduation lifecycle.
Backlog items are repo-only — they are not mirrored into OmniFocus until they graduate.
